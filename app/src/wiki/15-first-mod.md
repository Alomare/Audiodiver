This walks through replacing one piece of music, the loadout screen, and hearing it in game. HD2 Arsenal plays the result.

1. On [Home](app:home), choose **New project…**. Save the project in a folder of its own, with the audio files.
2. The Library opens. In the Index, open **Before the Drop** under Music and press ▶ next to **Loadout Selection**.
3. Click **Loadout Selection**: a start segment, then fourteen loops the game plays in order. Press **Add to the project**.
4. Go to the [Audio Editor](app:editor) and click **Loadout Selection** in the list.
5. Drag the audio files from Explorer onto the piece in the list, or onto the first slot. Slots fill in file-name order: name the files `01 …`, `02 …` and so on.
6. Select a slot and press ▶ on each side of its row: the original on the left, the replacement on the right. Switching keeps the position. The waveforms below show both on one time scale.
7. Next to **Play through** above the slots, press **Replaced** to hear the whole piece with the game's hand-overs. The slot that plays is selected as it comes.
8. On [Build](app:build), give the mod its name and icon.
9. Press **Build**. Import the ZIP in HD2 Arsenal, deploy it and start the game.

> [!TIP]
> Listen with the game's own volume settings, at the moments that changed. The game turns some screens down on purpose: see [Make-Up Gain and the game's mix](wiki:volume).

## What next

- Add more from the Library. A piece with several parts has **Fill the whole piece…**, which takes files for all its parts at once.
- Not only music: weapons, enemies, voices and the rest are in the Index too. See [Sounds and voices](wiki:sounds-and-voices).
- One long song? **Slice a song…** cuts it into pieces that play back seamlessly. See [Timing, cue points and loops](wiki:timing-and-loops).
- Changed something? Build again and import the new ZIP: Arsenal updates the mod in place. See [Building and installing](wiki:build-and-install).
