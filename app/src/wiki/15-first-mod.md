This page walks through replacing the music of one screen, the loadout screen, and hearing the result in game with HD2 Arsenal.

1. On [Home](app:home), choose **New project** and give the project a name. Audiodiver makes a folder for it in the projects folder (`Documents\Audiodiver\Projects` at first), with the project file inside. The [Project](app:project) tab opens, with the mod's name, icon and folder. Copy the audio files into that folder.
2. Go to the [Library](app:library). In the Index, open **Before the Drop** under Music and press ▶ next to **Loadout Selection** to hear it.
3. Click **Loadout Selection**. It has a start segment followed by fourteen loops that the game plays in order. Press **Add to the project**.
4. Go to the [Audio Editor](app:editor) and click **Loadout Selection** in the list.
5. Drag the audio files from Explorer onto the piece in the list, or onto its first slot. Slots fill in file-name order, so name the files `01 …`, `02 …` and so on.
6. Select a slot and press ▶ on each half of its row. The left half plays the original and the right half plays the replacement. Switching between them keeps the playback position. The waveforms below show both on one time scale.
7. Press **Replaced** next to **Play through**, above the slots. It plays the whole piece with the same hand-overs between segments that the game uses, and selects each slot as it plays.
8. On [Project](app:project), give the mod a name and an icon.
9. Press **Build**. Import the ZIP in HD2 Arsenal, deploy it and start the game.

> [!TIP]
> Listen in game with your normal volume settings, at the moments you changed. The game turns the music down on some screens. See [Make-Up Gain and the game's mix](wiki:volume) for details.

## Next steps

- Add more from the Library. A piece with several parts has **Fill the whole piece…**, which takes files for all of its parts at once.
- Weapons, enemies, voices and other sounds are in the Index too. See [Sounds and voices](wiki:sounds-and-voices).
- If you have one long song, **Slice a song…** cuts it into pieces that play back without gaps. See [Timing, cue points and loops](wiki:timing-and-loops).
- After you change something, build again and import the new ZIP. Arsenal updates the installed mod. See [Building and installing](wiki:build-and-install).
