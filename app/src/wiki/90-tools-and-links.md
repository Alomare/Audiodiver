## Installing and sharing mods

- [HD2 Arsenal](https://www.nexusmods.com/helldivers2/mods/4664): the mod manager Audiodiver builds for, on Nexus Mods. Its installers are also on [GitHub](https://github.com/Orbit-Studios/hd2arsenal-release/releases).
- [Helldivers 2 on Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/helldivers2): where most mods are shared.

## Editing audio

- [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org): a free editor for cutting, fading and exporting WAV or FLAC. Its *Loudness Normalization* effect sets a file to a LUFS target.
- [Youlean Loudness Meter](https://youlean.co/youlean-loudness-meter/): a free loudness meter, to compare new music with the game's −13 to −15 LUFS.
- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org): converts between almost any audio formats from the command line.

## Under the hood

- [vgmstream](https://github.com/vgmstream/vgmstream): plays and converts game audio formats, Wwise's included.
- [wwiser](https://github.com/bnnm/wwiser): explores Wwise sound banks.
- [hd2-audio-modder](https://github.com/RaidingForPants/hd2-audio-modder): an id-based audio modding tool for Helldivers 2.

What Audiodiver itself builds on is listed in [About](app:about) ({icon:info} next to the version, in the status bar).
