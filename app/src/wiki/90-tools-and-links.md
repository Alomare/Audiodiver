## Installing and sharing mods

- Builds install with a Helldivers 2 mod manager.
- [Helldivers 2 on Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/helldivers2) is where most mods are shared.
- **Audiodiver Utils**, on Audiodiver's [releases page](https://github.com/Alomare/Audiodiver/releases/latest), is Repatch and Combine Mods without projects: a smaller portable window, and `audiodiver.cmd` for the command line. `audiodiver.cmd repatch <mod>` rebuilds a mod, `combine <mod> <mod>` combines mods, `check` lists the installed mods that need a repatch, and `scan <mod>` shows the options of a mod. Add `--json` for output that other programs read, and `--help` for the rest.

## Editing audio

- [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org) is a free editor for cutting, fading and exporting WAV or FLAC. Its *Loudness Normalization* effect sets a file to a LUFS target.
- [Youlean Loudness Meter](https://youlean.co/youlean-loudness-meter/) is a free loudness meter. Use it to compare new music with the original level of −13 to −15 LUFS.
- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) converts between almost any audio formats from the command line.

## Under the hood

- [vgmstream](https://github.com/vgmstream/vgmstream) plays and converts game audio formats, including Wwise's.
- [wwiser](https://github.com/bnnm/wwiser) explores Wwise sound banks.
- [hd2-audio-modder](https://github.com/RaidingForPants/hd2-audio-modder) is an id-based audio modding tool for Helldivers 2.
