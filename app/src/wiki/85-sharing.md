## What to upload

The ZIP from the project's `build` folder, or the folder chosen for its builds. It is a complete HD2 Arsenal mod: players import it the same way.

On a mod page, say:

- which music it replaces (the ship, the drop, a faction, extraction…);
- which game version it was built on, so players know when it may need an update;
- what changed since the last version.

## Updates

Keep the mod's name and id the same for its whole life ([Build](app:build)). A player who imports the new ZIP then gets an update, not a second copy.

## Rights

- Share only music with the right to share it, and credit the artists.
- **Exports of the original** (**Export…** in the Library, or **Original** in the Audio Editor's exports) are for reference and mod work, not for upload.
