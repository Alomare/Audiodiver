---
title: Good practice
group: Making a mod
summary: Habits that keep a mod easy to test, update and share.
---

- **One folder per mod**: the `.audi` file, the audio and the `build` folder. A copy of the folder is a backup.
- **Name files in play order** (`01 …`, `02 …`): fills and slices follow it. A changed file exported again under the same name goes into the next build.
- **Work in passes**: rough in one area (the ship, the drop, one faction), build, play it, note gaps, abrupt cuts and quiet spots, fix them, build again. The loadout and drop-location screens are the quickest to reach in game.
- **Use Play through** before building: it catches most hand-over problems without starting the game.
- **Check the transitions** the Audio Editor flags with **!**. An original transition between two pieces of new music is the most common thing that sounds out of place.
- **Listen at quiet moments and in a firefight**: both matter for loudness.
- **Keep the mod's id and name** ([Build](app:build)) for its whole life, so Arsenal updates it instead of adding a second copy. **Make a new id** only when a copied project becomes a different mod.
