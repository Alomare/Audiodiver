---
title: Good practice
group: Making a mod
summary: Habits that make a mod easier to test, update and share.
---

- Keep **one folder per mod**, with the `.audi` file, the `audio` folder and `build`. A copy of the folder is a complete backup. If two projects share a folder, the [Project](app:project) tab shows a warning with **Move to a New Folder**.
- **Name the files in play order**, such as `01 …` and `02 …`. Fills and slices follow that order. If you export a changed file again under the same name, the next build uses the new version.
- **Work in passes.** Rough in one area, such as the ship, the drop or one faction, and build it. Play it and note gaps, abrupt cuts and quiet spots, then fix them and build again. The loadout and drop-location screens are the quickest to reach in game.
- **Use Play through** before you build. It catches most hand-over problems without starting the game.
- **Check the transitions** that the Audio tab marks with **!**. An original transition between two pieces of new music is the most common thing that sounds out of place.
- **Listen at quiet moments and during a firefight.** Loudness problems show up in both situations.
- **Keep the mod's ID and name** on [Project](app:project) for the life of the mod, so that the mod manager updates the installed mod each time. Use **Make a New ID** only when you copy a project to make a different mod.
