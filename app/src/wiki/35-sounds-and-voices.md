Music is the part of the game's audio Audiodiver knows best, but the Library has all of it. This page is about the rest.

## Sound sets

Outside the music, the Index lists **sound sets**: the sounds of one weapon, stratagem, enemy, voice or kind of planet, named the way the game names them to players.

A set's sounds show in groups: one sound with its **takes**, one of which the game picks at random each time. To replace a sound, give every take new audio, or the game will mix the originals in. Files dropped on a group's line fill its takes in order, and right-clicking the line fills, silences or clears them all.

Most groups are only numbered (*Sound 1*, *Take 2*: see [why](wiki:game-audio)), so listen: ▶ on a group's line plays its first take. In a big set the groups start folded, and **Filter these sounds** finds a sound by a word or an id. The **Other Audio** and **No Audio in the Game Files** groups are explained on the same page.

## Sounds many things share

The game keeps copies of some sounds in many places: a weapon's handling sounds are in the bank of every weapon. Such a group says **in 104 other banks** and comes after the sounds a set has to itself.

A replacement of a shared sound changes it everywhere it plays: that is how the game is built. The details list the other places under **Also plays in**. The mod also grows by a copy of each bank it touches, so replacing a widely shared sound makes a big mod.

## Voices

A voice has one part per installed language: *English*, *Italian* and so on. Each language has audio of its own, so a replacement in the English part reaches players who play in English, and nobody else.

Transcribed lines go by their words, and the filter finds them by those words.

*Helldiver Voice 1* isn't transcribed, so its lines borrow the words of another Helldiver voice (the four share one layout). Its own words may differ.

## All Archives

The Index only has what has a name. **All Archives** has everything: each archive with audio. What the Index knows shows as a name and its categories' icons; the rest says *Not named yet*. Sort by **Sounds** to find the big ones, or by **Language** to find a voice pack.

After a game update that brings new audio, look there: it can be heard, added to the project and replaced straight away.

Both lists take ids as well as words: paste a source id, a stream's file id or a bank's id, and the list shows where it is.
